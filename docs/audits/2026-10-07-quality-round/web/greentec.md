## What the sheet prints

Product name: "GreenTEC". The word "GreenTEC" is the only product name. **No base polymer or family is named anywhere on the sheet** (no PLA, PHA, PLA blend, copolyester or other). It says "Biokunststoff" (bioplastic), "100% nachwachsenden Rohstoffen", "nach DIN EN ISO 14855 biologisch abbaubar", "Für die Verarbeitung sind ähnliche Temperaturen wie bei PLA erforderlich" (PLA only as a processing comparison), "Die Wärmebeständigkeit ist mit 115°C (VST) relativ hoch." and "Höhere Temperaturbeständigkeit als PLA". Badge "100% biobased". "Dieses Filament erfüllt die Anforderungen an die Zusammensetzung der europäischen Verordnung Nr. 10/2011 über Kunststoffmaterialien für Lebensmittelkontakt." ## What is sold under the name elsewhere

The product is the same as Extrudr GreenTEC (Extrudr's own spelling "Green-TEC" / "GreenTEC"), which Extrudr calls part of its "BIO Performance range". Evidence: Extrudr's TDS https://3d.nice-cdn.com/upload/file/greentec-TDS-en.pdf (3DJake-hosted, dated 20. 06. 2024; saved at `scratchpad/q7/web14/ex2/greentec-TDS-en.pdf`, SHA-256 7f3c349fc7bd46f080cdb8910ec24398939b71b0096c179e6f3ec22b2c6153f2) prints the same figures as the purefil sheet and the same wording: "GreenTEC comes from our BIO Performance range and has been specially developed for high-performance applications. The material has a heat distortion resistance of 115°C (VST) and is optimised for high impact resistance. ... consisting of 100% renewable raw materials and biodegradable according to DIN EN ISO 14855. In addition, the raw material has food approval (FDA)." Values: Tensile modulus ISO 527 3200 MPa; Tensile strength 46 MPa; Elongation at strength 3 %; Nominal elongation at break (ISO 527-2) 14 %; Stress at break 18 MPa; Notched impact (ISO 179/1eA) 19 kJ/m2; Unnotched impact (ISO 179/1eU) 218 kJ/m2; VICAT A (ISO 3146-C, as printed) 115 °C; MFR 8 g/10min; Shrinking 0.5 %; Density 1.3 g/cm3; nozzle 200-230 °C, bed 20-90 °C. The 3DJake product page https://www.3djake.com/extrudr/green-tec-natur links that TDS ("Extrudr Green-TEC Natural, 1.75 mm / 1100 g"). Retailer text (search result, not fetched in full): "Green TEC is made of 100% renewable and naturally pure raw materials."

Conclusion: purefil GreenTEC is the Extrudr GreenTEC material resold under the purefil label (identical 3200 / 46 / 3 / 14 / 0.5 / 1.3 / 115 values). Neither sheet, neither shop page, nor the Extrudr TDS names the polymer; the only polymer words anywhere are the comparisons with PLA. A polymer label such as "PLA" would be an inference, not a statement of any source seen here. The two sheets differ in MFR (4 cm3/10min purefil, no temperature; 8 g/10min Extrudr, no temperature) and in the heat-bed range (50-90 vs 20-90 °C).
