# A filler only for looks: the products read

> **In short.** The owner ruled on 2026-10-20 that a variant whose filler is only for looks reads its type's printer
> guide in full (GOALS-LOG item 7; OPEN-PROBLEMS §32.4). The 28 active products filed "undisclosed dense filler" were
> read on their cached documents by Claude Opus 5.5. Eleven sell the product for a look a filler gives, a matte finish
> or a glitter, for no function, and say nothing of the filler changing how it prints: m470 files them "cosmetic dense
> filler", which prints as its type (D175). The other seventeen stay where they are, each for the reason below.

What they now read from their type's guide row (Bambu Lab's PLA or PETG HF column), where their own sheets and twins are
silent: the hardened-nozzle answer (not needed) for nine, the enclosure for seven, the chamber for four (not required,
from the guide's "Optional" enclosure) and drying for four (optional, 50 °C for 8 h). Spectrum PET-G MATT's own sheet
already answers everything, and Extrudr XPETG MATT's own "Enclosed Space for larger components" keeps its chamber
unknown (an enclosure asked for with no temperature has spoken).

## Left where they are

| Product | Grade | Why not a filler for looks |
|---|---|---|
| AzureFilm ABS Prime, ASA Prime | G027-39, G031-29 | Sold as a "zero warp engineering" filament whose "enhanced formulation" gives strength, impact resistance and interlayer adhesion: a function, and one that may print otherwise than the guide's ABS and ASA (which ask for an enclosure) |
| 3D4Makers Facilan C8 | G001-177 | Sold as an engineering formulation for manufacturing ("higher tensile strength than ABS", "the best layer adhesion"); its matte finish is one feature among them |

| colorFabb BronzeFill, Protopasta Brass/Bronze/Copper Fill HTPLA | G001-136, G001-139 | Metal powders (up to 250 µm, Protopasta); the same makers say their other metal fills wear brass, and D129 found the PLA guide telling a bronze fill that brass will do. | Nanovia PLA XRS | G001-135 | An X-ray shielding load |

| Recreus PLA Purifier | G001-184 | An air-purifying additive |

is unchanged.
