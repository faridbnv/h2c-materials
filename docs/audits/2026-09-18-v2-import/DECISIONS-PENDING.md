## Is a shop that hosts a sheet naming no maker the brand, or only the shop?

**114 document(s)**: 3DJake / 3DJAKE 79, MatterHackers / PRO Series 18, Filament2Print 17.

3DJake sells its own house brands beside other makers' filament, and a sheet it hosts that names no maker could be either. The pipeline will not guess, because a grade whose Manufacturer is the shop says the shop made it.

1. the shop is the brand where the product name is its own (3DJAKE easyPETG, Bulk PLA) and the maker otherwise
2. treat every such sheet as the shop’s own brand
3. hold them all until each is asked of the shop

Examples: 13 hyper PLA, 14 hyper PLA CF, 15 Hyper RFID PLA, 23 HYPER PETG, 3DJAKE ABS CF, 3DJAKE ABS Light Blue.

## What polymer is a product whose name says none?

**57 document(s)**: 3DJake / 3DJAKE 20, Fiberlogy 6, Filament2Print 6, BigRep 4, Extrudr 4, colorFabb 3.

A name like Facilan C8, easyPETG Pastel Pink or ReForm rTitan says nothing this reader can map to a row of polymers.csv, and D44 says a family owns no product.

1. a ruling per product
2. a ruling per maker where the maker’s range is one polymer
3. hold until the maker states it

Examples: Facilan C8 Filament, PI Filament Z2 Zymergen 3D4Makers, 3DJAKE easyPETG Pastel Pink, 3DJAKE ecoPLA CF, 3DJAKE mattePLA Silver, AmphoraAM3300.

## What holds a blend the sheet names by both its polymers?
