# Batch b16: QIDI, read by the labels around its rows

Applied 2026-09-20 by `m86-batch-b16`: 176 records from 13 documents. b14 held QIDI's whole library for this.

QIDI prints the property in Chinese on one baseline, the standard and the value on the next, and English on the
one after. Read as lines, "ISO 1183 1.07g/cm³" names no property at all; and the rows that did read took
whatever label had been held from further up the page, so a Young's modulus of 2,317 MPa was recorded as a
tensile yield strength and a heat deflection at 0.45 MPa as a Vicat point.

What m85 taught the reader, and what this batch is the proof of:

- the label beside a row beats one carried down to it;
- a label may stand on both sides of a row, and then neither half names the property alone;
- fifteen Chinese property labels, because a bilingual sheet says it twice;
- ISO 75's own title, "Determination of temperature of deflection under load";
- a load in brackets behind the value, without which both heat deflections stated no load and could screen no
  heat requirement (D65);
- and a sentence saying how the specimens were printed is not a result, which is what turned an infill of 100 %
  into an elongation at break.

## Sixteen documents are held, and one of them is worth explaining

| Held | Documents | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Several results on one row | 10 | the table heads "Method \| Molded \| X-Y Axis \| Z Axis" and prints three on a line |
| Read optically | 5 | a value read from a picture waits for somebody against the page image |
| Nothing left | 1 | its only row was rejected |

**The ten are a measured decision, not a guess.** Refusing a row that states several results in its own unit was
tried *inside the reader* and measured against every maker with hand-transcribed sheets: it cost **297 values the
database already holds**, across twenty makers, because a sheet prints two results on one line for good reasons
as often as bad ones. So the reader keeps reading them and the batch holds the document, with the count in the
ledger. Reading those columns by position is what would free them.

## What was decided rather than accepted

Three rows are rejected as misreadings. Two are sentences — "the stress range of the Z,X-axis modulus: 10~20MPa"
is the sheet talking about its own figure, and 10 MPa is a thousandth of what a PLA bends at. The third is a
label: the sheet lists an elongation at break and then a stress at 100 %, at 200 % and at 300 %, and the 100 in
the second one's label is not the first one's value.

Five findings are accepted with a reason apiece, four of them ASA-Aero's: a foaming filament prints at a fraction
of the density of the ASA it is made from, and its strength and stiffness fall with it.

One acceptance was removed because it no longer occurs — PBAT's tensile modulus stopped being an outlier once
this batch filled out the family around it.
