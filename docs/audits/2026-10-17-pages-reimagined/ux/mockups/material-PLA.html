In short

PLA is polylactic acid filament, sold as 195 products, a few of them filled. Makers describe it as easy to print, and prototypes and educational projects are among the uses they name. Most products need no heated chamber, many by the Bambu Lab Filament Guide. A few need a hardened nozzle, and most have drying advice from their sheets or the guide. It softens near its glass transition; its products' median HDT at 0.45 MPa is 55 °C. Of the makers that speak to acid and alkali resistance, most say not resistant.
