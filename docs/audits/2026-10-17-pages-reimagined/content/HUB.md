# Materials

A page for each of the 76 materials with enough published data: what it is, its typical properties across its products
and where they sit among the others, how to print it on the H2C, which products stand out, what makers say and what it
costs. To set requirements and get a shortlist, use the selector.

[Open the selector →]

Each figure is the median of the products that publish it; ¹ marks one product's value. *Bambu Lab* gives the
material's standing for the H2C.
