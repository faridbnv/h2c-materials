# The second research package (2026-10-19, round 2)

> **Historical record** (2026-10-20): kept from an earlier stage of the project. It describes the tool and its data as they were then; numbers, rules and file names may have changed since. For where things stand now, read the [README](../../README.md) and [OPEN-PROBLEMS](../../docs/OPEN-PROBLEMS.md).

The owner gave an external research agent a second brief, written from round 1's leftovers. It had ten tasks:
- T1: a third product for materials one short of a page;
- T2: makers' chamber statements;
- T3: missing headline values;
- T4: print recipes;
- T5: fibre loads;
- T6: printed-and-annealed pairs;
- T7: products for empty and thin materials;
- T8: prices;
- T9: identities;
- T10: document custody.

It worked outside the repository, against commit `53f266ec`, and changed nothing in it. It returned:
- 872 findings;
- 1,054 page readings;
- 220 saved files;
- 25 unsent handoffs.

Its EXECUTION-WARNINGS.md listed what had changed in the repository since then.

- **[disposition.csv](disposition.csv)** says what became of each finding:
  - *applied* (26), by the stage and migration named:
    - b50 (m488);
    - the readings (m489);
    - the filings (m490);
    - the review of the readings (m491, m492);
    - ASA's chamber row (m493);
    - the prices (m494);
  - *already in the data* (17): the page confirms the held record, or the copy is byte for byte a registered source;
  - *superseded* (2): D176 had settled them;
  - *left* (827), each with why:
    - scoped searches that found nothing;
    - document copies with other bytes (T10);
    - questions for makers or the owner;
    - products held for capture, stock or identity.
- **[handoffs.csv](handoffs.csv)** is the package's 25 handoffs as it wrote them. It is the list to send, and it is not
  data.
